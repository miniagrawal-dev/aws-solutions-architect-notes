# Common Interview Questions

### Bedrock vs SageMaker?

Bedrock provides managed foundation models for GenAI applications.

SageMaker is used to build, train, and deploy custom ML models.

---

### When should Textract be used?

When extracting structured information from scanned documents or forms.

---

### Why use Rekognition?

To analyze images and videos without building computer vision models.

---

### Why use Kendra?

To build enterprise search across multiple document repositories.
