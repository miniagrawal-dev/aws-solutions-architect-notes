# Interview Strategy

For every AWS interview question:

1. Explain what the service is.
2. Explain why you would choose it.
3. Explain when you would avoid it.
4. Compare it with similar AWS services.
5. Give a production use case.
6. Mention trade-offs.
7. Mention scaling considerations.
8. Mention security.
9. Mention monitoring.
10. Relate it to your real project experience whenever possible.

---

# One-Line Revision

Don't memorize AWS services. Learn **which problem each service solves, why it's the right choice, what the alternatives are, and how you've used similar concepts in real-world backend systems.**
